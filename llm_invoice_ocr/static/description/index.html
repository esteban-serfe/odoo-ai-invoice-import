Vendor Bill Import with AI for Argentina

Upload a supplier invoice (PDF or image) and get a draft vendor bill with the AFIP data already filled in, ready for your review.

Your data goes to the AI provider you choose. The invoice document is sent to the AI provider that you configure through your own LiteLLM proxy. This module does not send data to Serfe or to any fixed service.

What it understands

  • Is it really an invoice? The document is checked before anything is created.
  • Vendor CUIT, so the right partner is found. The importing company and its CUIT are named in the prompt so they are not confused with the vendor.
  • Voucher letter (A, B or C) and the matching Latam document type and number. For letters B and C every line gets VAT Not Applicable, as l10n_ar requires.
  • Point of sale and number as printed, and day-first dates.
  • The printed VAT rate, with its evidence, instead of a remembered one. Unusual rates such as 27% on telecom, gas or electricity are kept, because your company's purchase VAT rates are part of the prompt.
  • VAT percentage correction when the subtotal and total disagree with the per-line rate.
  • Perceptions (IIBB and VAT) applied as the matching AFIP tribute tax, and document-level charges.

Built to be reviewed

  • The AI output is a suggestion: check totals, taxes and the vendor before posting.
  • Best effort: any step that cannot be resolved leaves the draft as extracted. It never blocks the import.
  • Archive the Invoice Extraction assistant to switch AI import off. Upload then creates the bill with Odoo's standard decoders.

How to use it

  1. On a vendor bill or refund list, press Upload and choose the PDF or image.
  2. Review the draft in the wizard, or use Process with AI on an existing bill.
  3. Fix what is needed and post.

What you need

  • Odoo 16 Community, with l10n_ar for the Argentine behavior.
  • An AI provider account and a LiteLLM proxy in front of it, both provided and operated by you. LiteLLM works with the API of each provider: follow the LiteLLM and provider documentation to set it up.
  • In LLM → Configuration → Providers, create or open the LiteLLM provider, enter your proxy URL and API key, and press Fetch Models. Then assign a model to the Invoice Extraction assistant.
  • The OCA account_invoice_import modules, installed as dependencies.

Scope

Focused on Argentina (CUIT, voucher letters, AFIP taxes). Other countries are not covered: contact Serfe if you need them.
